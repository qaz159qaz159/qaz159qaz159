# Ming-Hao Hsu

I'm a PhD student at CUHK-Shenzhen, advised by Zhizheng Wu.
I build speech and audio language models and study why they can give
different answers to the same question when it is spoken or written.

[Website](https://mhhsu1006.github.io/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=OsbGc60AAAAJ)

## Selected work

- **ConvexLLM** — A general-purpose audio language model that maps audio into continuous mixtures of text embeddings. [Project](https://mhhsu1006.github.io/convexllm/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.23539)
- **Geometric Alignment without Functional Equivalence** · NeurIPS 2026 — A layer-wise study of where speech and text inputs lead to different answers. [Project](https://mhhsu1006.github.io/geoalign/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01502v2)
- **Is Text All You Need?** · NeurIPS 2026 — A speech-to-LLM interface built from convex combinations of frozen text embeddings. [Project](https://mhhsu1006.github.io/cgate/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09366)
- **ReListen** — Training-free evidence localization in hour-long audio with frozen audio language models. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.04480)
